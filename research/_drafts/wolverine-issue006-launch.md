## Discord Snippet (~270 words) — for #pillar-channel

**The Wolverine Stack: BPC-157 + TB-500 Under Audit — Issue 006 is live.**

If you've spent any time in peptide circles, you know the Wolverine stack. BPC-157 for local healing, TB-500 to "recruit repair cells systemically." Widely used — and for the first time, actually audited.

Here's what we found after verifying every source:

**BPC-157:** Repeated positive rat models — tendon, ligament, muscle. Real signal. But the only published human musculoskeletal report is one uncontrolled retrospective knee-pain series (11/12 reported improvement, telephone-reported, no objective testing).

**TB-500 is not full-length thymosin beta-4.** Mass-spec analyses identify commercial TB-500 as Ac-LKKTETQ — a seven-amino-acid fragment, chemically distinct from the 43-amino-acid parent. Evidence for one does not transfer to the other.

**The blend itself:** no controlled human or animal study comparing it against placebo. Zero. The "complementary mechanisms" story is a market narrative assembled from separate findings.

**Product reality:** recalls, endotoxin, purity failures in independent testing — the vial is part of the intervention.

**Process note:** our verification caught two fabricated ClinicalTrials.gov records that looked like real 2026 trials. They're excluded and disclosed. This is what "Look closer. Claim less." means in practice.

The honest read: the people using this aren't fools. They're ahead of the research. The next step is a real study — not a better argument.
